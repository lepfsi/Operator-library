# PART II

## THE AUTOMATION CURVE

*The handover feels reasonable, one step at a time*

No one decides to remove the operator. The system is simply allowed to do one more thing.

The curve begins with routine because routine is easy to call waste. It moves into diagnosis because recognition feels like assistance. It reaches action because speed appears to be safety. By the time a runbook is deciding, the transfer of authority has already happened in increments too small to look political.

> Automation rarely arrives as a replacement. It arrives as the next reasonable permission.

The chapters ahead trace each permission to the authority it quietly relocates.
