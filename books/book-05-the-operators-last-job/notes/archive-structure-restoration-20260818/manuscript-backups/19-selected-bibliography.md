# Selected Bibliography

The sources in this bibliography support the book’s documented incidents, technical claims, and conceptual frames. It is deliberately selective. Each entry has either informed a cited passage, established the factual basis of a scene, or offers a durable path for further reading.

The book follows a notes-and-bibliography approach: numbered notes locate a source near the relevant claim, while this consolidated list gives readers a stable route back to the works that shaped the argument. Web sources include an access date because operational documentation and incident reports can change after publication.

## A Note on Evidence

A documented incident is cited in a chapter note and recorded here when it informs the book’s argument. An **operational composite** is not listed as an incident because it is a constructed, credible scenario designed to illuminate a mechanism. A prospective scene is likewise identified in the prose rather than presented as an established case.
