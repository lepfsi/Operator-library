# Editorial architecture

## Thesis

> What happens when execution becomes cheap but judgment remains scarce?

The book studies the redistribution of operational work as systems gain the ability to observe, diagnose, recommend, and act. It rejects both a simplistic replacement narrative and a complacent “human in the loop” solution.

## Reader promise

By the end of the book, readers will understand what remains deliberately human in IT operations: judgment, responsibility, context, trust, and the decision not to act.

## Guardrails

The manuscript does not sell a product, predict job-loss figures, or turn AI into a generic villain. Every chapter must ground its claim in operational consequences and, before it reaches draft status, name at least one real incident from `case-library.md`.
