**Question.** What remains when the system is under pressure and the playbook is no longer enough?

Part II exposed the recovery paths, hidden dependencies, and temporary decisions beneath the visible system. Part III turns to the people who must operate that reality. These chapters examine judgement under pressure, knowledge held by individuals, documentation that fails when it is needed, and the human choices that shape a system long after an incident is closed.

The technical system is only half the operating environment. The other half is what the team can understand, decide, and do when certainty disappears.
